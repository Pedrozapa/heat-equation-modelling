# heat-equation-modelling
# Analytical and Numerical Study of the Heat Equation

## Overview

Academic group project developed during my Erasmus+ year at Wrocław University of Science and Technology, within the MSc programme in Applied Mathematics.

The project explores the one-dimensional heat equation from both a theoretical and a computational perspective, combining mathematical analysis with numerical simulations in Python.

## Mathematical Background

- Parabolic partial differential equations (PDEs)
- Separation of variables and Fourier series
- Existence and uniqueness of solutions
- Homogeneous Dirichlet boundary conditions
- Mathematical modelling of thermal diffusion

## Numerical Methodology

- Finite difference methods
- Forward Time Centered Space (FTCS) scheme
- Numerical stability conditions
- Spatial and temporal discretization

## Simulations

The numerical implementation illustrates the evolution of temperature along a one-dimensional rod over time.

The results are visualized through temperature profiles at different time steps and a three-dimensional representation of the diffusion process.

## Technologies

Python, NumPy, Matplotlib and Jupyter Notebook.
